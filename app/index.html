The thirteen systems, and what each one found

Every figure below is computed from a published government record and carries the coverage it rests on. 13 of 13 systems have measured output; 32 of 44 registered sources returned data to an unauthenticated request on the last run. Computed 2026-09-11.

  • Catchment — School place planning at the geography that matters.

    89.5% of mainstream school places in use — 633 specialist settings over capacity

  • Sentinel — Public procurement integrity and collusion detection.

    6.2% of awards skipped open competition — across 2,203 award records

  • Highwater — Flood risk against what actually got built.

    635 permissions granted against flood advice — of 23,336 objections; 7,011 outcomes never recorded

  • Plumbline — Housing delivery measured against the real deadline.

    15.9% decided within the legal deadline — published headline is 82.8%

  • Junction — Grid connection capacity on one definition.

    937 of 6,770 capacity records actually served — 1 of 4 operators serve data openly

  • Ledger — Developer contributions traced to the site.

    £1.49bn of developer contributions recorded — 0 of 39,325 carry a location

  • Bellwether — Provider concentration in care and education.

    124 authorities depend on one care group — Welltower — 37,404 beds across 72 companies

  • Sightline — Whether expert planning advice is followed.

    177 water quality objections — none carry a recorded outcome — the field does not exist

  • Lastmile — Gigabit compliance for new homes.

    83.3% gigabit in new-build postcodes — against 83.5% everywhere else

  • Bulwark — Who owns and maintains flood defences.

    11,919 inspections overdue — maintainer known on 91% of 141,469 defences

  • Watchman — Insolvency exposure across public suppliers.

    0 exposures found in the sample — the register must accumulate before this produces signal

  • Compass — SEND demand forecast by neighbourhood.

    +127.4% growth in statutory EHC plans — 236,806 to 538,547 since 2015

  • Baseline — Sewage spills normalised for rainfall.

    300,406 spills after adjusting for monitor uptime — 291,492 reported across 14,239 outlets

READING PLATFORM OUTPUT…

UK government records what happened.
It rarely records where, or which organisation.

A flood objection with no location. A billion pounds of developer money that can't be put on a map. A care company quietly running a third of a county's beds. The data exists — but two joins are missing, so nobody can use it.

UK GroundTruth adds those two joins — where and who — to data anyone can download, without an account or a fee. That single fix makes thirteen things possible that are impossible today. Every figure below is computed from published records on one machine. Nothing is estimated.
